About this data and methodology

Candidate fundraising combines supplied 2025 annual reports with 2026 30-day and 8-day primary reports for the same committee account. “Total receipts” is the sum of Schedule A receipts across non-overlapping periods: annual reports end Dec. 31, 2025; 30-day reports cover Jan. 1 through Aug. 16, 2026; and 8-day reports cover Aug. 17 through Sept. 7, 2026. “Candidate loans” totals candidate-named Schedule D-1 loans across those same non-overlapping supplied periods; debts attributed only to vendors are excluded, and the figure is not the committee’s current outstanding loan balance. Candidates without every report reflect only the periods supplied to Spotlight Delaware. Ending balance is taken from the latest available report.

The in-state comparison uses the mailing state printed for itemized Schedule A contributors across the included periods. Filing rows labeled “TOTAL OF CONTRIBUTIONS NOT EXCEEDING $100” generally do not identify individual donors or addresses; this database displays those aggregate receipt rows and classifies their amounts as in-state donations. Other receipts without an itemized address are shown separately.

Third-party advertiser spending is drawn from Schedule B expenditures in the supplied filings through Sept. 8, 2026. It is never added to a candidate committee’s fundraising. Candidate totals include only expenditures that explicitly name the candidate or allocate an exact amount to that candidate.

PAC profiles are published only when the supplied PAC reports show expenditures greater than $0. Direct payments to named candidate committees are labeled as PAC donations on PAC profiles, but are not added to “Additional Outside Spending” because candidate committees may also report those donations in Schedule A. PAC administrative and other unattributed spending remains part of the PAC’s reported expenditure total without being assigned to a candidate.

Advertiser profiles aggregate those candidate-attributed expenditures by candidate and preserve the filing’s stated position. Spending labeled as opposition is displayed as benefiting the opposing candidate rather than supporting the candidate targeted by the advertisement.

When a filing explicitly opposes a candidate in a two-person primary, the expenditure is shown as a benefit to the sole opponent and labeled “Opposition to …” If a race has more than two candidates—or the filing’s candidate allocations do not reconcile to its expenditure—the money remains unassigned rather than being guessed.

Amended reports replace original reports covering the same period, and duplicate downloads are counted once. Contributor locations are limited to the city and state reported in the filings; street addresses and ZIP codes are not published. Names and locations may contain filing-entry errors. A contributor can appear more than once; search results show transactions, not deduplicated people.
